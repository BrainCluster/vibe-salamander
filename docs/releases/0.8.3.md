**Vibe Salamander 0.8.3**: shortcuts through the clipboard: Copy as shortcut, and Paste shortcut everywhere it belongs.

## New

- **Copy as shortcut** (Ctrl+Shift+L, also in the right-click menu and Edit): shortcuts to the selected items go on
  the clipboard, so Paste (Ctrl+V) makes them anywhere, in Vibe Salamander or in Explorer
- **Paste shortcut** (Ctrl+S; Ctrl+Shift+V with Total Commander's keys) is in the right-click menu whenever files
  are on the clipboard, on items and on a folder's empty area

## Better

- **Files copied in Vibe Salamander can be pasted as shortcuts in Explorer**: the desktop's and Explorer's menus now
  offer Paste shortcut for them, as for files copied in Explorer
- **The Windows menu of a folder's empty area** (and of "..") is Explorer's own: Paste shortcut, New, Properties, ...
